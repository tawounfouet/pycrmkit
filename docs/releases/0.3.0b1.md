# PyCRMKit 0.3.0b1 — Pipeline & Stage

This beta adds configurable sales pipelines and policy-driven Opportunity stage movement.

## Added

- immutable `Pipeline`, `Stage`, and `StageTransition` definitions;
- normalized stable pipeline/stage keys and ordered unique stage positions;
- terminal stages with `won`, `lost`, or `cancelled` outcomes;
- finite Decimal probability defaults, including deterministic terminal defaults;
- explicit `InvalidStageTransition`;
- `PipelineTransitionPolicy` with initial-stage, terminal-stage, and allowed-transition enforcement;
- backend-neutral `PipelineRepository` and `PipelineService`;
- official copy-isolated `MemoryPipelineRepository`;
- Pipeline participation in `MemoryStore` / `MemoryUnitOfWork`;
- `crm.pipelines.define/get/list`;
- `crm.opportunities.move(...)`;
- Opportunity `stage_entered_at` and current-stage duration metric;
- post-commit `pipeline.created`, `opportunity.stage_changed`, and terminal outcome events with transactional audit history;
- unit, repository-contract, UoW, facade, E2E, compatibility-smoke, and installed-wheel qualification.

## Deliberately deferred

`crm.leads.convert(...)` remains scheduled for `0.3.0b2`, where Lead state change and Opportunity creation can be made atomic and idempotent.
