# PyCRMKit 0.3.0a1 — Money & Lead Foundation

This alpha opens Phase 3 / Sales Foundation without introducing Opportunities, Pipelines, Stages, or public lead conversion.

## Added

- immutable Decimal-only `Money` with required normalized currency;
- `LeadId`, `LeadStatus`, and Lead lifecycle states `new`, `open`, `contacted`, `qualified`, `disqualified`, `converted`;
- typed Contact linkage and optional Organization linkage;
- backend-neutral `LeadQuery`, `LeadRepository`, and `LeadService`;
- copy-isolated `MemoryLeadRepository`;
- Lead participation in `MemoryStore` and `MemoryUnitOfWork`;
- `crm.leads.create`, `crm.leads.qualify`, and `crm.leads.disqualify`;
- `lead.created`, `lead.qualified`, and `lead.disqualified` events with audit integration;
- reusable repository contracts plus unit, adapter, transaction, facade and end-to-end qualification.

## Explicitly deferred

The following are not part of `0.3.0a1`:

- Opportunity domain (`0.3.0a2`);
- Pipeline and Stage model (`0.3.0b1`);
- public/idempotent lead conversion (`0.3.0b2`);
- Sales end-to-end release-candidate freeze (`0.3.0rc1`).

The stable `0.1` and `0.2` contracts remain unchanged.
